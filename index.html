Askling H-Test

Straight-leg raise · iPhone IMU

Protocol and mounting
  1. With the athlete supine, secure the upper body and opposite leg. Brace the tested knee in full extension (0°). Use no warm-up exercises.
  2. Perform the passive flexibility assessment first. Slowly raise the straight leg until the athlete reports a strong but tolerable stretch.
  3. Firmly secure the iPhone to the brace, screen facing up at rest, long edge aligned with the shin, top of phone toward the ankle. Verify retention before testing. Enable iPhone Portrait Orientation Lock if needed.
  4. Use one submaximal practice raise, then consecutive scored raises as fast and high as the athlete can safely perform. Return to the start between raises. If comparing injured and uninjured sides, test the uninjured side first.
  5. Record pain and insecurity after each set. Stop if the athlete reports symptoms requiring the test to stop or the mount shifts.

Results estimate leg excursion relative to the starting position with the pelvis stabilised. Use within a clinician-supervised assessment. The app provides no return-to-sport decision.

Positioning: trunk rotation

Seated. Screen facing upward. Place one long edge of the iPhone against the sternum. Keep the device against the thorax and minimise independent arm movement. Stabilise the pelvis according to your protocol.

Interpretation

The trunk mode measures rotation of the phone's long axis projected onto the horizontal plane. It does not measure thorax–pelvis separation. Joint ROM from zero describes phone orientation, not automatically anatomical joint angles.

Sensor limitations

Web APIs may use an arbitrary or magnetic orientation reference, update at varying rates, and be suspended when the app is backgrounded. A tilt warning threshold of 15° is a configurable engineering heuristic, not an established clinical cut-off.
